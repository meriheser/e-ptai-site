6a. Walking Across 81 Provinces weekly step competition

The competition first runs only in Türkiye's 30 metropolitan provinces; the other 51 provinces open in later stages. E-PtAi is the organiser.

  • Joining: free and optional, for people aged 18 and over. You compete in one province a week and can change it every 4 weeks.
  • Dates: the week runs from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (Türkiye time).
  • What counts: only sensor-verified steps. Manual entries and a second record of the same walk do not count.
  • Ranking: by steps only, with no element of chance. On a tie, whoever reached the total first ranks higher, then whoever joined first.
  • Gifts: the gift is one month of AI Coach for free, added to the winner's account after approval; it is digital, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be passed on. Only the first place of each province wins, and one person can win only one gift a week. Results are final after a person reviews suspicious activity and two staff members approve.
  • Appeals and alternates: a high step count alone is never treated as cheating. You can appeal within 7 days of a notice. Someone confirmed to have cheated loses the gift, and the next verified participant is considered.
  • Gift delivery: there is no physical product or shipping, and no address or phone number is asked; tax obligations are met under the applicable law.

Apple Inc and Google LLC are not sponsors of this competition and are not involved with it in any way.
