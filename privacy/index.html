3. Legal bases and how we collect data

You enter some of your data yourself in the App's forms (a non-automated method). The rest arrives by automated means: your phone's motion sensor, Apple Health / Health Connect with your permission, sign-in with Apple or Google, and device and app details.

  • Entering into and performing a contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): account and sign-in; your profile details (sex, birth year); verifying subscriptions and lesson credits; setting up the coach link, the member number and lesson planning; delivering your gift if you win one.
  • Explicit consent (KVKK Art. 6(3)(a); GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)): body details and progress photos; step, workout, meal, water and weigh-in records; the Apple Health / Health Connect link; sharing the sections you choose with your coach; joining the step league, reviewing your step records for cheating and showing your name, province and step total to other members; sending the questions you type, the details you enter, the machine photo and the plate photo in the AI features to Google's AI service (Gemini); processing of this data by our service providers abroad (for safeguards see section 6). Each permission is asked for on a separate screen.
  • Legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): security, bug fixing and preventing abuse; the device integrity check and device digest in the step league; showing and measuring non-personalised ads to free members (in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland your permission for device access is asked for separately, see section 7). We never process special category data on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Legal obligation (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)): lawful requests from competent authorities; tax and accounting records if a gift is awarded.

You can withdraw a permission at any time in Profile → Privacy or on the screen concerned (for coach sharing, turning off the sharing switch is enough; for the league, leaving the league is enough). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

4. Special rules for health data

  • Your health and activity data is never used for advertising or marketing and never shared with ad companies.
  • Apple Health data is handled under Apple's HealthKit rules and is never sold to third parties.
  • Manually entered steps do not count in the step league.
  • The AI features work only with your explicit consent. The AI provider is Google (Gemini API, paid tier; inputs are not used for model training). Only the following goes to the provider: the questions you type into Ask AI and the earlier answers in the same chat; for the AI program, your goal (lose, keep or gain weight), number of training days per week and level; a single machine photo in "Recognise a machine with the camera"; a single plate photo in "Snap your plate, count the calories". If you also gave explicit consent to the processing of your health data, your health summary goes to AI Coach so it can tailor its answer: sex, age, height, latest weight, target weight, body fat, waist, daily activity, today's and this week's steps. Your name, e-mail, progress photos and past measurement records are not sent. If you type health information into a question, that information goes to the provider too; we advise you not to type health details that identify you.
  • The App does not diagnose or treat any condition. See a doctor if you have a health problem.

Step league: Joining is optional and starts only with your separate consent. Your display name, province and weekly step total are shown to other members in that province's ranking. Suspicious records are flagged by automated signals. No one is disqualified on the basis of an automated result alone; an authorised person decides, and you can appeal within 7 days of the notice. Your contact details for a gift are asked for only after the gift is confirmed, and they are stored encrypted. You can leave the league at any time.

4a. Location and the walk map

  • What: your phone's GPS location, only during a walk you start and until you finish it (also with the screen locked). The purpose is to draw your route on the map, work out distance, time and pace, and show your own heat map.
  • Legal basis: explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1, GDPR Art. 6/1-a). The consent screen comes before the location permission and is off by default; you can withdraw it at any time with "Withdraw location consent".
  • Where: your routes are stored only on this phone, linked to your account; they are not sent to our server, not shared with anyone and never go to ad networks. A route is not health data, but because it can reveal a visit to a hospital or a place of worship we apply the same strict rules as for health data.
  • Map: map images load from OpenFreeMap servers; with each request your IP address and the area you look at reach that provider (international transfer, KVKK Art. 9; basis: explicit consent). No account data is sent.
  • Retention: the last 100 walks stay on the phone; they are deleted one by one, with "Delete all my walks" or completely when you delete your account. The walk map is not available to anyone under 18.

We do not sell your data.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers are outside Türkiye: hosting (Supabase, EU: Frankfurt (eu-central-1)), RevenueCat (USA) and Google (AdMob and the Gemini API; the USA and other countries where Google has facilities).

Users in Türkiye (KVKK Art. 9, as amended by Law No. 7499):

  • Processing condition: your explicit consent for your health and activity data (KVKK Art. 6(3)(a)); the performance of the contract or our legitimate interest for your other data (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c), 5(2)(f)).
  • Safeguard: the standard contract announced by the Personal Data Protection Board is signed without changing its text. It is notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority within 5 business days of the signatures being completed. The additional measures taken for health data are set out in the contract.
  • For regular transfers (hosting, AI requests, subscription verification), explicit consent does not replace the standard contract. If we ever rely on your explicit consent for a one-off or occasional, non-regular transfer, we explain the possible risks to you separately in advance.

Users in the EEA (GDPR Art. 44-49): You send your data through the App directly to us (Türkiye). For our transfers to service providers outside the EEA, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision (EU 2023/1795) for Google LLC in the USA, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (EU 2021/914) for the other providers. If hosting is inside the EEA, sending the data from there to us also relies on these standard contractual clauses. Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 49(1)(a)) is used only for non-repetitive, occasional transfers. You can ask for a copy of these safeguards at destek@e-ptai.com.

7. Ads

  • AI Coach members see no ads.
  • Free members may see rewarded videos (watching is your choice) and native in-app ads.
  • Ads are requested as non-personalised. Your advertising ID is not used to track you across other apps and websites, so the App does not show the iPhone tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency).
  • Non-personalised ads may still use your advertising ID and device details for frequency capping, aggregate measurement and preventing ad fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) opens before the first ad. There you can allow or refuse storing and reading information on your device. If you refuse, Google can show only limited ads.
  • You can change or withdraw your ad choices at any time in Profile → Privacy → "Ad privacy options".

8. Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is open.
  • If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days. Records we are required by law to keep, activity logs kept for security (3 months) and backups (90 days) are kept for the relevant period and then deleted; details are on the e-ptai.com/delete-account page.
  • Step league: the gift is digital, so no address or phone number is asked from winners; information that tax law requires us to keep is kept for the period set by that law. Hourly step records, cheating signals and appeal records are deleted or anonymised 90 days after the week is final and appeals are closed.
  • Data on your device (profile photo, preview records, the province you chose for the weather and the last weather answer (for up to one hour) and progress photos) is removed when you delete the App.

9. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11, GDPR)

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to:

  • learn whether your data is processed and, if so, request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate, and for it to be erased or destroyed under KVKK Article 7;
  • ask for those corrections and erasures to be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred;
  • object to a result against you that arises from an analysis made solely by automated systems;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

Users in the EEA can also, under the GDPR, get a copy of their data, ask for processing to be restricted and receive their data in a portable format.

  • Right to object (GDPR Art. 21): You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest. This covers: security, bug fixing, preventing abuse, the device check in the league and non-personalised ads. We stop that processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Withdrawing consent: You can withdraw any permission you have given at any time in the way described in section 3. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
  • Deleting your account: Profile → "Delete Account". If you cannot open the App, you can also ask at e-ptai.com/delete-account. That page sets out what is deleted and what is kept, and for how long.
  • Requests: You can make your request in one of these ways: by a letter with your wet-ink signature to Beşevler Mah. Yıldırım Cad. Yılmaz Apt. Sitesi No: 274/1 C, Nilüfer/Bursa; by registered electronic mail (KEP), secure electronic signature or mobile signature to matmer@hs01.kep.tr; or by e-mail to destek@e-ptai.com from the e-mail address registered in your account. Your request must include: your full name (and your signature for a written request), your Turkish ID number (if you are a foreign national, your nationality and passport or ID number), your address for notifications, your e-mail address and phone number if any, and your request. Requests are made in Turkish; we also answer requests that arrive in other languages. We resolve your request free of charge, as soon as possible depending on its nature and within 30 days at the latest. If the action involves an additional cost, only the fee set by the Board may be charged: TRY 1 per page for a written reply of more than 10 pages, and the cost of the medium for a reply given on a recording medium.
  • Complaints: You must first apply to us. If your request is rejected, you find the reply insufficient or you get no reply within 30 days, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Art. 14). The deadline is 30 days from the date you learn of the reply, and in any case 60 days from the date of your request. If you live in the EEA, you can also complain to the data protection supervisory authority of the country where you live, work or where you believe the infringement took place (GDPR Art. 77). In Spain this authority is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es). List of all EEA authorities: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en. If you live in the United Kingdom, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. On the server, row-level security (RLS) rules mean each user sees only their own data and the data others have chosen to share with them. We never store passwords in plain text.

11. Children

The App is not designed for anyone under 18 (our AI provider's terms also require this). If we learn that we hold data of a user under 18, we delete it.

12. Changes

If we update this policy, we announce it in the App and at e-ptai.com. We notify you separately in the App about important changes before they take effect. If we are going to process your data for a new purpose, we inform you about that purpose in advance. If the new purpose relies on your explicit consent, we ask for that consent in a text separate from this one.
