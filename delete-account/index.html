E-PtAi account and data deletion

The E-PtAi app is provided by Granola Kozmetik Medikal ve Tekstil Sanayi Ticaret Pazarlama Limited Şirketi. You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open E-PtAi and sign in.
  2. Profile → "Delete Account".
  3. Confirm with "Yes, delete permanently". Deletion starts at once and cannot be undone.

Before you delete:

  • If you have an active App Store or Google Play subscription, first cancel it in your store account's subscription settings. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription (see section 5).
  • If you own a gym or PT business account, you first need to hand the business over to another manager or close it; for help, write to destek@e-ptai.com.

2. If you cannot open the app

Send an e-mail with the subject "Account deletion" to destek@e-ptai.com from the e-mail address of your account. We complete your request within 30 days at the latest and tell you the result by e-mail. We never ask for your password.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account details: name, e-mail, account type
  • Your profile and body details and your measurements
  • Your progress photos: stored only on your device; deleted when you delete the app from your phone.
  • Your workout, meal, water, step and weigh-in records
  • Your display name on the leaderboard. In the rankings of past weeks that are already final, your name is replaced with "Deleted member"; the province, rank and step count stay without a name and can no longer be linked to you.
  • The access of your linked coach and gym ends immediately.
  • Other personal records linked to you on our servers (except the exceptions in section 4)

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Your personal data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days of your deletion request at the latest.
  • We notify the service providers we transferred your data to about the deletion. Your record with our subscription verification provider, RevenueCat, is deleted too.
  • Activity logs kept for security and to prevent abuse (account number, type and time of the action; no name, e-mail or health data) are kept for 3 months, then deleted.
  • The league gift is digital (one month of AI Coach), so no address or phone number is asked for it. Tax and accounting documents for the gift are kept, with restricted access, only for the period set by the law concerned (10 years, Turkish Commercial Code art. 82); they are deleted when that period ends.
  • Copies in backups are deleted through the backup cycle within 90 days at the latest; backups are not used during that time.
  • If we cannot fully carry out your request for a legal reason (for example, an accounting record the law requires us to keep), we tell you the reason in writing or electronically within 30 days at the latest.
  • We never hold your card details; your purchase records stay with Apple and Google under their rules.
  • Data on your device (profile photo, preview records) is removed when you delete the app from your phone.

5. Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. To avoid further charges, cancel it separately in your store account's subscription settings. Because the subscription is tied to your store account, you can still cancel it there after you delete your account.
