Your AI-powered personal
fitness and nutrition coach

E-PtAi is a mobile app that builds a personal workout program, a weekly meal plan and daily tracking around your goal, body measurements and lifestyle. Its AI coach reviews your progress every day and updates your plan.

iOS and Android – coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Features

Personal workouts

Pick the body areas to train; it plans sets, reps and time for the gym or home at your level.

Nutrition plan

Daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets and a weekly menu built on Turkish cuisine.

Plate photo

Snap your meal and see an estimate of its calories and macros.

AI coach

Daily voice and text motivation, progress feedback and plan suggestions.

Muscle map and measurements

See how much each muscle group is trained and how your weight and measurements change.

Smartwatch and health

Sync steps and activity with Apple Health and Health Connect.

Pricing

Free

Workout, nutrition and BMI tracking are free.

Premium

Advanced charts and extra programs; subscription prices are shown in the app store.

AI Coach

Personal AI coach subscription, managed in the app and cancellable at any time.
