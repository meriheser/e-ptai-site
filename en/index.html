Questions

When does it launch?

Soon. The buttons on this page open when it reaches the App Store and Google Play.

Is it free?

You start free; only the AI Coach is paid. No ads for the first 30 days. After that, free members watch one short rewarded video a day to open the move videos for that day, and see one ad card on the Progress screen. AI features can also be opened for the day with a short video. AI Coach members see no ads.

How much is the AI Coach?

₺99.99 a month or ₺719.99 a year. One subscription, no trial. Cancel in your store account.

Does it give medical advice?

No. E-PtAi is not a medical device and offers no diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health problem, see your doctor.

Does it work with my smartwatch?

Preparing for smartwatch integrations.

Does this site collect data?

No. This site has no ad or analytics cookies and collects no health data. For data in the app, see the Privacy Policy.

Which languages?
