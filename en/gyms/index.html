For Gym

Your gym, your members and your bar in one app.

Member check-in, programs, classes, announcements and the Vitamin Bar account are inside the same E-PtAi app your members use. There is no separate gym app.

  • ₺999/month
  • First month free
  • 200 active members
Do you work one-to-one? Check the Fitness Coaches page.

Set up the gym

At first sign-up, you choose the Gym role. Setup has three steps.

  1. Gym info

    Gym name, city, and optionally a gym phone.

  2. Plan

    ₺999/month, first month free. No payment or card required.

  3. Invite code

    The code and QR you give your members will be ready. You can renew the code anytime; the old code stops working immediately.

Add your members and track them

You approve join requests; the panel gives you a summary of the day.

Join by code

The member enters the member code or scans the QR, enters their phone number; you approve. Wrong code attempts are limited to 10 per hour.

Add by phone number

Add one by one by phone or paste a list (up to 500 rows). Choose the duration: 30 days, 90 days or 1 year. Split members into groups.

Renew, freeze

Renew membership with +30 days. Freeze for 7 to 60 days (max 90 days per year); while frozen, check-in and classes stay closed.

Daily panel

Four numbers: today's check-ins, memberships ending today, orders and pending approvals; tap a number to open its list. Below is a single “Who should I call?” list: people who haven't come for a long time and memberships ending within 7 days. The list is read-only; calling or messaging is your decision.

Membership packages, candidates and records

Small tools for your Gym's daily tasks. The app does not take payments; you collect payment at the Gym.

Membership packages and renewal

Write the package name, number of days and price. When you assign it to a member, the end date moves forward. You record the payment in the ledger as cash, card or bank transfer; the app does not take payments and does not ask for card details. Packages ending within 7 days appear in the renewal list, and you can send the member a notification in the app.

Participation text

You paste the Gym's participation text; every change is a new version. Each member reads and approves each version once; the approval is stored with date and version, and you see how many members approved it. The Gym and its lawyer prepare the text; the app does not provide a legal e-signature and does not add health questions.

Candidates

Save a candidate who wants a trial with name, phone and optional trial day. When they become a member, make them a member with one tap. You see how many candidates became members and the “who brought me” count; this is only a count, there is no reward.

Private lesson packages

Give a member a package such as “10 lessons”; each lesson deducts one, and both you and the member see how many lessons are left. The number of lessons in the last 30 days is tracked per coach. It is only a count; there is no commission calculation.

Export as a table

You get members, check-ins and payments as a table (CSV). Only name, phone, membership, check-in and payment records are exported; health data and what members share with permission are not included. Only the Manager can export. It does not replace an invoice.

Write from WhatsApp

Each member row has a WhatsApp button; it opens a short message on your own phone. There is no bulk sending; in-app messages remain separate.

QR at entry

The member's phone becomes their digital member ID.

Member's QR

The member opens the entry QR in the app; the code refreshes every 30 seconds and contains no personal information. Reception scans it and the entry is recorded.

Door kiosk

The tablet or phone at the door becomes a kiosk: it either shows the door code for members to scan (changes every 30 seconds) or reads the member's QR with the camera.

Manual search

If there is no camera, search by name or the last 3 digits of the phone number and record the entry.

Backup code

If you want, you can also issue a printed entry code. It does not count as proof of entry on its own; you check it at the desk.

Programs and classes

Videos come from the app's movement library: more than 370 movements.

Share a program

Choose movements, enter sets and reps, or start from three ready-made programs. Publish to all members, selected members, or a group. The program disappears when completed; a member never sees a half-finished program.

Member does it in the app

The member watches today's workout with videos and taps “I completed it.” You see how many people completed the program today. Videos are available as long as the membership continues.

Class schedule

Publish a group class: day, time, duration, capacity. The member reserves a spot; if the class is full, they join the waitlist. Attendance opens one hour before the class.

Nutrition plan

Share a daily menu from the foods in the meal builder. It is general information; it is not a personalized diet or medical advice.

Announcement and message

You reach your members inside the app.

Announcement

Write a title and text of up to 500 characters; send it to everyone or to a group. There are ready-made texts for holidays, class cancellations, and renewals. The announcement appears only inside the app.

Message

Send a reminder or campaign message to all members, a group, or a single member; see how many people opened it. Only the sender and the recipient read the message; it is not used for advertising.

Members absent for a long time

Lists members who haven't come by number of days and prepares a “We missed you” message with one tap. It is calculated only from check-ins and program marks.

Vitamin Bar

Bar sales are added to the member's account. You collect payment at the gym; there is no card or payment in the app.

Sales and account

Scan the member's QR and add the product to their account. When payment comes in, record the amount as cash, card or bank transfer. The member sees their current balance and account activity in the app; refunds and adjustments are recorded.

Products and prices

You enter the products and their prices; you can remove any product from sale.

Orders, discounts and hot food

Ordering from the app

The member selects a product and places an order. In the queue, you move it from new to preparing to ready; when it is ready, the member gets a notification. At pickup, the member shows their QR and the amount is charged to their account at that moment.

Instant discount

Put a discount on a product as a percentage or a new price, until an end time. Members see it and get a notification. Each gym can post at most 2 discount announcements per day; the member can turn off notifications at any time.

Hot food

Mark a product as hot food; the member chooses 1 or 1.5 portions. You enter the price for 1.5 portions.

End-of-workout question

10 minutes before the member's workout ends, if they entered the gym with a QR that day and the gym is open, they are asked, “Shall we prepare hot food?” The workout information stays on the phone; the gym only sees the order if the member says yes. It is asked once a day, and the member can turn it off.

Roles and reports

Enter a staff member's email address and assign their role. Everyone sees only their own work.

Manager

Everything: dashboard, members, program, announcements, messages, Vitamin Bar accounts, staff, and reports.

Reception

QR scanning, check-in, member approval and renewal, kiosk, Vitamin Bar sales and payment. Cannot see plans and reports.

Trainer

Reads check-ins, publishes classes, shares programs. No sales, payment, or reports.

Gym in numbers

Active and frozen member count for the last 7 or 30 days, check-in count, check-ins by day and hour, busiest hours, class occupancy, and Vitamin Bar sales. Numbers only: no names, health, weight, or measurements. Only the manager sees prices.

Member data belongs to the member

The gym sees only what it needs to do its job.

What the gym sees

Name, phone, membership, check-ins, and whether the program you gave is completed.

If the member allows it

The member can turn on three sections one by one: training days, weight and measurements, steps. All are off by default; the member can turn them off at any time, and every change is versioned and saved. Only the Manager and Trainer see open sections; Reception never sees them.

The gym never sees

Nutrition and photos, plus any section the member keeps closed. There is also no free-text note field in the member record.

AI form check

If the member wants, they check their movement on their own phone with AI. The camera image does not leave the phone; the gym only sees the “done” mark.
