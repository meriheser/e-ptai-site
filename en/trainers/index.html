For Fitness Coach

Your students, programs and classes in one place.

As a Fitness Coach on E-PtAi, you invite your students, build programs from movement videos, manage class times and message. All inside the same app your students use.

  • ₺299/month
  • First month free
  • 50 active students
Do you run a gym? Check the Gyms page.

Sign up, set up, invite

On first sign-up, you choose the Fitness Coach role. Your coach space is ready in three steps.

You can change your role in Profile at any time; no data is deleted.

  1. Coach info

    Enter your display name, specialty, city, and phone number.

  2. Plan

    ₺299/month, first month free. No payment or card required at setup.

  3. Invite

    Your invite code and QR code for your students will be ready.

Add your students

Nothing opens until you approve.

Code or QR

In Student Profile > Join Gym, they enter the invite code or scan the QR, then enter their phone number.

Add by number

Add phone numbers one by one or paste a list (up to 500 lines). If you want, split students into groups.

Approve, remove

You approve join requests. If you remove a student, their access closes immediately.

Remaining slots on screen

You always see your active student count and how many student slots you have left.

Build the program, give it to your students

You build the program from the app's move library: there are videos for more than 370 moves.

From scratch or with a template

Choose moves day by day, enter sets and reps. Or start with one of three ready-made programs: beginner, upper and lower body, yoga and pilates at home.

Choose who gets it

Publish it to all students, selected students, or a group. It is sent when complete; a student never sees half a program.

Student does it in the app

The student watches today's workout with videos and says 'I completed it'. Videos are available as long as they are a student.

Nutrition plan

You can prepare and share a daily menu from the foods in the meal builder. It is general information; it is not a personal diet or medical advice.

Classes and appointments

Publish group classes and open your free times for one-to-one lessons.

Group class

Write the class name, day, time, duration and capacity, then publish. The student reserves their spot with one tap; if the class fills up, they join the waitlist and move up when a spot opens. Attendance opens one hour before the class.

One-to-one lesson appointment

Enter your free time slots day by day. The student sends a request for a time; you accept, reject or suggest another time. You can cancel a confirmed lesson or postpone it by suggesting a new time.

No fee

Appointments have no lesson fee, payment or cancellation penalty. Times are in Turkey time (UTC+3).

Message and see who took a break

You talk with your students in the app.

Bulk message

Write to all students, a group, or a single student. There are ready texts for “We missed you”, renewal, and a new class; you see how many people opened it.

One-to-one chat

You chat with the student. Only text is sent; no photos, videos, or files. There is blocking and reporting.

On a break

A list of students who haven’t marked the program for a long time is created; you prepare a message with one tap. It is calculated only from program marks; health data is not read.

Your students in numbers

Number of active and frozen students in the last 7 or 30 days, and class fill rates.

What you see, what you don't

A student's data belongs to the student.

You see

Name, phone, membership status, and whether they checked the program you gave.

If the student allows

If the student turns on the sharing switch on the my measurements screen, you see their weight info. The switch is off by default; the student can turn it off anytime.

You don't see

Health data, measurements (unless the student shares them), workout content, and camera footage. AI form check runs on the student's phone; you only get a “done” mark.
